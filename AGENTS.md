# CodeBau Design and Development Principles

## Inspired Functional References
When developing features, workflows, and pages for CodeBau, use as functional inspiration (without copying design, text, code, or trademarks):

1. **HORNBACH Germany (hornbach.de)** for B2C & Project-Oriented Experience:
   - Project-based shopping & calculators
   - Category navigation & rich product detail pages
   - Click & Collect (reservation & local store pickup)
   - Delivery logistics & service booking
   - Step-by-step DIY project guides and instructions
   - Mobile app experience & seamless store-to-online integration

2. **RAAB KARCHER Germany (raabkarcher.de)** for Professional & B2B Experience:
   - Meister Club & Corporate B2B account management
   - Real-time local & regional store stock availability
   - Deep technical specs, datasheets, & DoP documents
   - Job site / construction logistics & scheduling
   - Trade tools, planning estimators, & pro quoting
   - Contract pricing, bulk volume orders, & pro conditions

## Regional & Brand Adaptation (Republic of Moldova - South Region)
- **Target Region**: Southern Republic of Moldova (Stores: Cahul, Cantemir, Vulcănești, Taraclia + 24/7 Lockers)
- **Currency**: Moldovan Leu (`MDL`), formatted using `ro-MD` locale (e.g., `74,90 MDL`)
- **Languages**: Romanian (`ro`) primary, with Russian support
- **Brand Identity**: Dark Navy / Slate Blue (`#0f172a`), Clean White, and CodeBau Vibrant Orange (`#f97316` / `#f59e0b` accents)
- **Core Philosophy**: CodeBau does not just sell materials — CodeBau empowers clients and pros to plan, calculate, purchase, deliver, and execute complete construction & renovation projects.

## Mandatory Quality & UX Checklist Before Completing Any Screen
- Clear and practical for B2C project DIYers
- Rich technical depth for B2B professionals & Meister Club members
- Transparent local store stock & store selector
- High-contrast, clear primary commercial actions
- Fully responsive across desktop (1440px) to mobile (360px)
- Maintains distinct CodeBau brand identity
